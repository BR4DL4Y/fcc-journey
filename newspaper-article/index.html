Hero of Time Returns in Stunning New Remake!

Ocarina of Time breaks sales records on Nintendo Switch 2.

By Malon from Lon Lon Ranch

The timeless classic has been reborn with completely overhauled visuals, orchestrated music, and voice acting. Link sets out once again on an epic quest through Hyrule to defeat Ganondorf.

With more than 5.2 million copies sold in its first four days, it has officially become the fastest-selling game on the Nintendo Switch 2 platform.
