#  Newspaper Article - The Hyrule Times

An online newspaper article project designed with **HTML5** and **CSS3**, developed as part of my responsive web design learning path on freeCodeCamp.

This special edition simulates a Hyrule newspaper front page covering the anticipated release of the *Ocarina of Time* remake for the new Nintendo Switch 2.

##  Article Content

* **Newspaper:** The Hyrule Times[cite: 1]
* **Headline:** Hero of Time Returns in Stunning New Remake![cite: 1]
* **Topic:** Launch sales records and graphical upgrades featuring Latin American Spanish voice acting.
