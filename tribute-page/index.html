The Rise of Mortal Kombat!

The Evolution of a Fighting Game Icon

Created by Ed Boon and John Tobias in 1992, Mortal Kombat transformed the gaming industry with digitized martial artists, deep lore, and unapologetic intensity.

  • 1992: Original arcade release introduces Scorpion, Sub-Zero, and Fatalities.
  • 1993: Home console ports spark Senate hearings, leading to the creation of the ESRB rating system.
  • 1995: First live-action film becomes a worldwide box-office success.
  • 2011: NetherRealm Studios reboots the franchise with Mortal Kombat (MK9).
  • 2019: Mortal Kombat 11 surpasses 15 million copies sold.
  • 2023: Mortal Kombat 1 launches a brand-new timeline.
